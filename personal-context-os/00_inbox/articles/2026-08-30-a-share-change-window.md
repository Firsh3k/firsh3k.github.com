## Distilled insights

### 1. Capital Equilibrium / Funding Stalemate
Type: FRAMEWORK + LESSON  
Decision: PROMOTE TO EXPERIENCE POOL  
Authority: observational, non-gating

When several major funding channels show weak or mutually offsetting inflow/outflow, the market can enter a fragile equilibrium in which neither side is willing or able to force direction. This state should raise attention to a possible future direction-selection window, but it does not predict whether the break will be upward or downward.

User-approved A-share experience wording:

> 场内的钱既不进来，也不出去，大家就这么僵着。这种平衡本身，恰恰是变盘前最典型的形态。

PCOS interpretation: treat this as an experience heuristic, not a deterministic law.

### 2. Volatility Compression Regime
Type: FRAMEWORK + STRATEGY_CANDIDATE  
Decision: PROMOTE TO OBSERVATION LAYER  
Authority: non-directional, non-gating

Falling implied volatility and/or realized volatility can indicate reduced disagreement and compressed range. Extreme or persistent compression may precede range expansion. Direction must still be resolved using existing Market State evidence: technical structure, R/A/D, breadth, turnover, weekly/monthly context and sector structure.

### 3. Up/Down Volume Asymmetry
Type: FRAMEWORK + STRATEGY_CANDIDATE  
Decision: PROMOTE TO OBSERVATION LAYER  
Authority: non-gating

Repeated low-volume rebounds combined with higher-volume selloffs may indicate weak active demand and stronger downside urgency. The opposite pattern can support a healthier accumulation interpretation. Compare a sequence of sessions rather than one isolated day.

### 4. Crowd Positioning / Sentiment Survey
Type: HYPOTHESIS  
Decision: OBSERVE ONLY

High positioning + extreme optimism may imply limited incremental buying capacity; low positioning + extreme pessimism may imply more upside optionality. Reader surveys are sample-biased and must remain auxiliary evidence only.

### 5. Overseas catalyst lesson
Type: LESSON / DEDUP  
Decision: REFERENCE ONLY

Strong overseas earnings or guidance can be a catalyst, but cannot by itself confirm an A-share trend or authorize a trade. This is already substantially covered by the current Market Radar catalyst-quality + T+1 persistence framework.
